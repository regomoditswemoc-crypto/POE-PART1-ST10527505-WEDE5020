About Sunrise Bakery

Sunrise Bakery was founded in 2015 by Nomvula Dlamini in Roodepoort, Johannesburg. It started as a small home-based baking business and grew into a shop after two years because of steady demand from the surrounding neighbourhood.

Our Mission

To bake fresh, affordable bread and cakes for families and local businesses in Roodepoort every day.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted bakery brand in Johannesburg, known for quality baking and friendly service.
